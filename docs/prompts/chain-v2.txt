CHAINS CATEGORY RULES. Standalone neck chain is the hero. Preserve length, link shape/width/thickness/pitch, visible pattern sequence, rigid stations, strands, end fittings, connectors, visible clasp, finish and asymmetry. Never extrapolate unseen link counts. One chain stays one even if a reference shows several; two runs of a loop are not two products. Preserve real multi-strand joins and approved products; exclude incidental products/reflections.

No invented pendant, charm, central drop, bead, stone, extra strand or matching product. Preserve genuine unexpected components rather than deleting jewellery to force category conformity. Never copy reference designs/counts, substitute a generic chain, fuse links, fill openwork or turn links into a ribbon. Hidden/cropped ends or clasps must not be invented. Tag removal must preserve fine metal links and actual findings.

Pose changes only through genuine articulation: dimensions, length and component order stay fixed. Adapt support/framing, not chain length. Keep open ends open; no invented closure or compulsory deep U. Rigid stations stay rigid; joints bend plausibly. No sharp kinks, impossible twists/crossings, duplicated loops or fused strands. Top runs need real support; lower runs follow gravity or rest naturally. No adhesive-looking placement or invented hooks. Suspended sections may cast offset shadows, not fake contact outlines. Keep supported chain, ends and clasp fully readable; no crop. Preserve fine edges/openings, sharp defining links and source metal tone; coloured displays must not recolour jewellery. No reference flowers/leaves/rocks/fans/branding.

SCENE 1 — WHITE ARCHITECTURAL STEPS. Bright premium studio with two or three broad matte neutral-white stepped plinths, gently softened edges and a continuous white background. Drape the source chain across an upper landing, over a broad step edge, into a relaxed curve on the lower landing. For a closed construction use a traceable elongated loop; for open construction preserve ends in an open curve. Select span/height for the actual length and rigidity; keep the supported product complete and readable. Never reproduce the reference's multiple-chain assortment. Elevated three-quarter view reveals upper surfaces, front faces and edge transitions. Moderately directional upper-left light with controlled softness and fill; clear but restrained shadows. Shadows on a vertical face reflect real chain-to-face spacing, not a glued-on outline. Protect polished highlights. No marble veining, coloured border, text or unrelated props. Prioritize product readability over a complicated staircase.

SCENE 2 — CHARCOAL CURVED DISPLAY. Broad matte charcoal rounded support on a continuous dark neutral surface, subtly tactile rather than glossy. Place the upper chain run on the crest; lower runs follow the curved support into a relaxed elongated foreground loop, or a source-supported open curve. Use a broad radius compatible with rigid stations or flat articulated links. Keep each genuine source product readable; do not duplicate a single chain to copy the two reference products. Modestly elevated diagonal view, controlled neutral upper-left key and restrained fill. Keep fine links separated from dark surfaces without bright halos. All supported ends, clasp and lower turn remain visible and sharp. Gold remains source-accurate, not exaggerated orange. No copied thick openwork design, seller logo, loose rocks or decorative fan. The support visibly carries the chain; no suspended cylinder-like pipe or unsupported jewellery.

SCENE 3 — EMERALD VELVET ROLLS. Deep emerald/teal-green velvet with two or three broad horizontal upholstered rolls or soft ridges. Short realistic pile, subtle directional texture and tonal variation; no satin/plastic shine. Drape the source chain over the upper roll, down the contours into a relaxed lower curve resting on foreground velvet; preserve genuine open ends. Adapt roll radius/height to actual chain length and articulation. Slight pile compression at contact; do not bury fine links, fill openings with fibres, deform rigid stations or wrap cloth around metal. Near-front, slightly elevated view; softly directional upper-left light and neutral fill. Whole supported product stays in the square, sharp through the lower curve; only distant background may soften. Keep green reflection subtle, metal tone accurate. No reference flowers, purple branches, dried palm leaves or invented pendant. Velvet remains secondary to the chain.

SCENE 4 — BURGUNDY GEOMETRIC BLOCKS. Deep burgundy studio background with one primary wine/magenta block and at most one secondary block for depth. Matte/fine satin planes, subtly softened edges, restrained saturation; no fluorescent pink or glossy plastic. Top run rests on the primary block; sides descend in front into a source-compatible lower curve. Preserve open ends when applicable. Select block height/width for the actual chain length; no stretching or shortening. Hanging runs must be carried by the supported top run, not adhered to the vertical face. Front-oriented, slightly elevated camera reveals top support and modest block depth. Controlled upper-left directional key, moderate neutral fill and restrained side separation; crisp source-supported metal detail without pink recolouring. Shadows distinguish actual contact from offset projected shadows. Fit complete supported product into the square without a large empty portrait header. No second chain copied from reference, logo or typography.
