PURPOSE

Transform the wholesaler's raw jewellery photograph into a premium, studio-quality jewellery photograph while preserving the EXACT original jewellery product.

IMPROVE THE PHOTOGRAPH, NOT THE PRODUCT.

The source jewellery is the approved product. Do not redesign, reinterpret, modernize, beautify, simplify, complete, or replace it with generic jewellery.

PROCESS:
RAW SOURCE
→ PRODUCT IDENTIFICATION
→ COMMERCIAL REMOVAL
→ PRODUCT FIDELITY LOCK
→ SOURCE CORRECTION
→ PROFESSIONAL PHOTOGRAPHY
→ CATEGORY PHYSICAL PRESENTATION
→ FINAL QC

The category module supplied after this prompt controls ONLY category-specific physical behavior and presentation. This base controls all universal rules.

4. Physical/structural realism
5. Source-supported detail only
6. Product readability and completeness
7. Lighting and photographic quality
8. Background and styling

Never sacrifice product fidelity or completeness for aesthetics.

Never invent jewellery to improve an image.

3. RESTING PHYSICS — ABSOLUTE

The ring must behave like a small rigid object naturally placed on soft fabric.

Do not create artificial gaps, magnetic suspension, glue-like attachment, floating, deep penetration, or unstable balancing.

The final result should look as though the ring was gently placed on the fabric and naturally came to rest.

4. FABRIC INTERACTION

The fabric responds to the ring; the ring does not deform to the fabric.

Do not create deep dents, excessive wrapping, fabric passing through the ring, mechanical wrinkles, artificial attachment, or impossible contact shadows.

Keep the textile continuous and physically believable.
