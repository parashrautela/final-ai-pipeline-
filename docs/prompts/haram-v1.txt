 HARAM

Apply these rules specifically to HARAM jewellery while preserving all universal base-prompt rules. Haram is a long neck piece; retain the established neck-piece presentation logic.

1. COMMERCIAL MARKING REMOVAL — ABSOLUTE

Remove all non-jewellery commercial material, including price tags, price stickers/labels, barcodes, SKU/inventory/category labels, product cards, paper tags, seller markings, promotional text, printed numbers/codes, watermarks, website/social handles, commercial logos not physically manufactured into the jewellery, and tag strings, clips or holders.

PRICE TAG REMOVAL IS ABSOLUTE. No price, barcode, SKU, commercial text, label, watermark, or tag may remain. Never preserve, blur, crop, hide, relocate, recreate, or reinterpret commercial markings. Remove the complete physical tag and its attachments. If removal exposes background, reconstruct only the minimum surrounding area. Never invent jewellery hidden by a removed tag.

2. HARAM PRODUCT FIDELITY — ABSOLUTE

The source Haram is the exact approved product. Preserve its:
- Overall silhouette and full vertical length
- Width, proportions and number/order of layers
- Chains, strands, links and connectors
- Repeated motifs, temple/deity motifs and iconography
- Gemstones, beads, pearls and filigree
- Engravings, borders, relief, openings and cavities
- Central pendant and side ornaments
- Danglers
- Original metal construction and intentional asymmetry

Do not redesign, simplify, modernize, beautify, add, remove, duplicate, or rearrange jewellery elements. Do not make it more symmetrical than the source.

3. LONG HARAM / U-SHAPE PHYSICS

Treat the Haram as a long U-shaped neck piece. Preserve the exact source-supported U depth, vertical drop, side curvature and central lowest point.

The Haram must:
- Fall naturally under gravity
- Maintain a continuous U-shaped silhouette
- Transition smoothly toward the central lower section
- Keep upper ends at believable attachment points
- Avoid unnatural stretching, compression, flattening or widening

Do not convert it into a shallow V, shorten its drop, flatten the U, or alter its source-defined silhouette. Keep the full length visible within the universal 1:1 composition.

4. MULTI-LAYER / MULTI-STRAND INTEGRITY

If multiple chains, layers or strands exist, preserve their exact count, order, spacing and hierarchy.

Each strand must remain physically distinct. Do not merge strands, turn multiple chains into one band, duplicate or remove strands, randomly change spacing, or allow one strand to pass impossibly through another.

5. WEIGHT & GRAVITY

Respect the physical weight of dense gold sections, central ornaments, pendants, deity motifs, beads and medallions.

Weight should create believable downward tension through the long necklace. Chains and strands must not float, remain rigid without structural reason, or stretch beyond their construction.

Gravity must never distort the original Haram silhouette.

6. DANGLERS, BEADS & LOWER ORNAMENTS

Preserve the exact number, size, spacing and arrangement of beads, pearls, chains, drops and danglers.

Each must hang from its actual attachment point. They should fall naturally downward with slight physically plausible variation.

Do not make danglers float sideways or point upward without physical reason. Do not merge them, duplicate them, remove them, or allow them to clip through one another.

7. CENTRAL ELEMENT

The central lower element must remain at the natural lowest point and retain its exact source-supported:
- Pendant/medallion
- Deity or figurative motif
- Gemstones
- Borders and relief
- Beads/pearls
- Connectors and supporting elements

Keep it physically attached to the Haram. Do not make it float, detach it, excessively rotate it, or change its front-facing orientation.

8. CHAIN & CONNECTION INTEGRITY

Every chain, link, connector, bead, ornament and pendant must remain physically connected.

No broken continuity, floating links, detached ornaments, missing connectors, merged links, chains passing through ornaments, impossible intersections, duplicated components, or invented attachment points.

Preserve the source connection sequence. Fine chains must remain fine chains; never convert detailed links into generic smooth metal strips.

9. DISPLAY & COMPOSITION

Follow the universal neck-piece presentation logic.

The Haram should be:
- Hung vertically
- Fully visible from top to bottom
- Uncropped
- Presented with its long portrait-weighted character preserved inside the 1:1 frame
- Naturally aligned with gravity
- Presented against the approved luxury environment from the base prompt

Do not place it flat on a table, fold it, compress it, shorten it, or present a long Haram as a short necklace.

10. SILHOUETTE LOCK

During background replacement and enhancement, preserve the exact outer silhouette, U depth, side positions and central lower contour.

Do not erode, expand, warp, smooth away structural details, or change the relative position of the two sides. Commercial-tag removal must never alter the actual jewellery silhouette.

11. FINAL VALIDATION

Before finalizing, verify:
- Full Haram visible and original length preserved
- U-shaped silhouette and curvature preserved
- Layer/strand count and spacing preserved
- All chains and connectors remain continuous
- Danglers hang naturally
- Central ornament remains attached and correctly positioned
- No jewellery is invented, duplicated, omitted or redesigned
- No price tag, label, barcode, SKU, inventory/category marking, watermark, seller/website/social marking, pricing text, paper tag or string remains
- No commercial marking has been transformed into jewellery
- The final Haram is unmistakably the exact source product
