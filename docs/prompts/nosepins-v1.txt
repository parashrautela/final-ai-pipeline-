 NOSEPIN

Apply these rules specifically to nosepins while preserving all universal base-prompt rules. The source nosepin is the exact approved product.

IMPORTANT CATEGORY OVERRIDE:
For NOSEPIN products only, a human nose is the approved presentation surface. The nosepin must be shown naturally worn on a real human nose. The face is context only; the nosepin remains the sole jewellery product and receives highest fidelity priority.

1. HUMAN NOSE PRESENTATION — ABSOLUTE

Never present the nosepin:
- Flat on fabric
- On a tray/table/stone/cushion
- Floating independently
- As an isolated product cutout
- Without a human nose

Show enough of the nose and nearby facial area to establish natural wear, but keep the nosepin clearly dominant.

The nosepin must look physically worn, not digitally placed onto a face.

2. ANATOMICAL PLACEMENT

Position the nosepin at a believable nostril piercing location.

For a conventional stud:
- Decorative face sits on the outer nostril surface.
- Stud is flush or naturally seated against skin.
- Metal/post enters the piercing plausibly.
- Decorative face maintains a readable orientation.
- Do not place it on the bridge, cheek, nose tip, or inside the nostril unless the source specifically requires it.

The jewellery must be anchored to the actual skin surface, not floating above it.

3. SCALE & PROPORTION

Nosepins are small-scale jewellery. Preserve the exact source scale and proportions relative to the human face.

Maintain believable relationships between:
- Nosepin diameter
- Stone size
- Border
- Metal thickness
- Nostril size
- Nose width

Do not enlarge the jewellery to make it more visible or shrink it until details disappear.

It must look like real small jewellery worn by a real person, not an enlarged ornament.

4. SKIN CONTACT & ATTACHMENT

The nosepin must physically contact the nose.

For studs:
- Decorative front sits naturally against the outer nostril.
- Post/metal enters the piercing plausibly.
- No artificial gap unless required by the source construction.
- Attachment direction follows the nostril surface.
- No floating or pasted-on appearance.

Avoid excessive skin indentation or deformation. The jewellery should appear seated against the skin, not embedded unnaturally.

5. NOSE CONTOUR & ORIENTATION

Follow the three-dimensional curvature of the nostril.

Do not force the jewellery perfectly flat toward the camera if the nose requires a slight angle.

Balance:
- Natural nostril curvature
- Realistic piercing direction
- Camera visibility
- Original jewellery orientation

Do not excessively rotate or distort the nosepin. The nose adapts to the jewellery's placement; the jewellery geometry remains unchanged.

6. STUD NOSEPIN PHYSICS

For stud-style nosepins, preserve the exact structure of:
- Central stone
- Surrounding stones
- Prongs
- Metal border
- Decorative motifs
- Post/back structure where visible

Keep the central stone centered within its original setting.

Do not let stones sink into skin, detach settings from posts, enlarge stones, or alter the relationship between components.

The complete stud must read as one physically assembled jewellery piece.

7. NOSE HOOP / RING VARIANT

If the source is a nose hoop/ring:
- Preserve its exact circular or intentionally shaped geometry.
- Maintain original diameter and thickness.
- Follow the nostril's natural curvature.
- Keep the structure continuous.
- Enter and exit the piercing at physically plausible points.
- Keep it naturally seated against the nose.

Do not flatten, stretch, deform, detach or unnaturally intersect the hoop with the nose.

It must look like real metal passing through a real piercing.

8. HUMAN FACE REALISM

The visible face must provide realistic physical context.

Preserve natural:
- Skin texture
- Subtle pores
- Tonal variation
- Facial curvature
- Highlights and shadows

Do not make skin plastic, wax-like, CGI-like or unnaturally smooth.

Avoid excessive retouching that destroys the physical relationship between jewellery and skin.

The nosepin remains sharper and more visually important than surrounding facial texture.

9. HAIR & OBSTRUCTION

If hair is present, it must behave naturally and maintain realistic depth.

Do not allow hair to:
- Pass through the nosepin
- Cover the nosepin
- Artificially hide jewellery imperfections

The nosepin must remain fully readable.

10. JEWELLERY FIDELITY

Preserve the exact source:
- Silhouette
- Diameter
- Stone count
- Stone arrangement
- Gemstone colors
- Metal color
- Setting
- Prongs
- Borders
- Engravings
- Filigree
- Surface texture
- Relief
- Proportions

Do not simplify small details.

Do not replace tiny stones with larger ones, add/remove stones, change settings, smooth intricate construction, or redesign the jewellery.

The source product is authoritative.

11. LIGHT & MATERIAL INTERACTION

Metal highlights must follow the actual three-dimensional geometry.

Gemstone reflections must correspond to real facets and settings.

The nosepin may create a subtle contact shadow where it meets the skin. Keep the shadow physically attached and consistent with the universal lighting.

Do not create artificial halos, excessive shadows or independent glowing jewellery.

The jewellery must appear illuminated by the same environment as the face.

12. COMMERCIAL MARKING REMOVAL — ABSOLUTE

Remove ALL non-jewellery commercial material, including:
- Price tags/stickers/labels
- Barcodes
- SKU/category/inventory labels
- Product cards/paper tags
- Printed or handwritten pricing
- Numbers/codes
- Seller markings
- Watermarks
- Website/social handles
- Promotional text
- Commercial logos not physically manufactured into the jewellery
- Strings, clips or holders used for such markings

PRICE TAG REMOVAL IS ABSOLUTE.

No price information may appear anywhere.

Never preserve, blur, crop, hide, move, recreate, replace or reinterpret commercial markings.

Remove the complete commercial object and attachments. Reconstruct only the minimum exposed background.

Never confuse commercial markings with jewellery details.

13. FACE COMPOSITION & PRODUCT PRIORITY

Show enough nose anatomy to make the wearing context immediately clear.

Do not frame so widely that the nosepin becomes insignificant or crop so tightly that the nose anatomy becomes confusing.

Maintain realistic jewellery scale while giving the nosepin meaningful visual presence.

THE FACE IS CONTEXT.
THE NOSEPIN IS THE PRODUCT.

14. FINAL VALIDATION

Verify:
- Nosepin is naturally worn on a real human nose.
- Placement matches a believable nostril piercing.
- Jewellery physically contacts skin.
- No floating or pasted-on appearance.
- Original scale, geometry, stones, metal and details preserved.
- Stud is correctly seated or hoop follows the nostril.
- Jewellery follows natural nose curvature without deformation.
- Metal/gemstone reflections match the established lighting.
- Contact shadow is subtle and physically attached.
- Hair does not obscure or pass through jewellery.
- No jewellery is invented, removed or duplicated.
- No price, barcode, SKU, label, watermark, seller marking, commercial text, tag, string, clip or paper edge remains.

FINAL RULE:

THE NOSEPIN MUST BE SHOWN BEING NATURALLY WORN ON A HUMAN NOSE.

THE NOSEPIN'S ORIGINAL DESIGN AND SCALE ARE NON-NEGOTIABLE.

REALISTIC NOSE-TO-JEWELLERY CONTACT IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.

PRICE TAG REMOVAL IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.

COMMERCIAL MARKING REMOVAL IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.

WHEN UNCERTAIN:
PRESERVE THE SOURCE.
DO NOT INVENT.
