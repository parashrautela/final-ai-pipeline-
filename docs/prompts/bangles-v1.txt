BANGLes

Apply these rules specifically to BANGLES while preserving all universal base-prompt rules. The source bangle is the exact approved product.

The bangle is a rigid jewellery object presented resting naturally on premium soft, textured velvet or suede-like textile. Do not redesign, reinterpret, simplify, or materially alter the original product.

1. COMMERCIAL MARKING REMOVAL — ABSOLUTE

Remove ALL non-jewellery commercial material:
- Price tags/stickers/labels
- Pricing/numbers/codes
- Barcodes
- SKU/inventory/category labels
- Product cards/paper tags
- Seller/store markings
- Promotional text
- Watermarks
- Websites/social handles
- Non-product commercial logos
- Strings, threads, clips and holders used for identification

PRICE TAG REMOVAL IS ABSOLUTE.

No price, barcode, SKU, category marking, seller marking, watermark, promotional text or commercial attachment may remain.

Never preserve, blur, hide, crop, relocate, recreate, replace or reinterpret commercial markings. Remove the complete tag and attachments. If removal exposes background/fabric, reconstruct only the minimum surrounding area. Never invent jewellery hidden by a removed commercial object.

2. BANGLE FIDELITY — ABSOLUTE

Preserve the exact source bangle:
- Overall silhouette
- Outer/inner circumference
- Diameter
- Band thickness and width
- Cross-sectional profile
- Decorative patterns and repeated elements
- Gemstones and placement
- Motifs and borders
- Engravings, grooves, ridges and filigree
- Relief, openings and closures
- Surface texture
- Structural joints
- Handcrafted irregularities

Do not redesign, simplify, beautify, make more ornate or more symmetrical, add/remove stones, change gemstone size/color/placement, or replace the source with a generic luxury bangle.

The source is the product, not inspiration.

3. RIGID CIRCULAR GEOMETRY — ABSOLUTE

The bangle is rigid.

Preserve its exact circular, oval or intentionally shaped geometry.

Do not:
- Bend
- Flatten
- Stretch
- Compress
- Warp
- Distort the inner opening
- Change circumference
- Change band thickness
- Turn circular geometry into oval
- Make a rigid bangle behave like a flexible bracelet

The bangle remains rigid; the textile conforms subtly to it.

If the source has an intentional non-circular shape, preserve that exact shape.

4. VELVET / SUEDE PRESENTATION

Present the bangle on premium soft textile such as:
- Velvet
- Suede-like fabric
- Soft brushed textile
- Fine tactile cloth

The universal base controls the fabric color.

Use natural wrinkles, folds, soft ridges, gentle depressions and subtle surface texture. The fabric must look soft and compressible, not plastic or perfectly synthetic.

The textile remains secondary; the bangle is the dominant product.

5. BANGLE-TO-FABRIC PHYSICS

The bangle must behave like a rigid physical object naturally resting on soft fabric.

It must obey gravity and settle into a stable position.

Do not allow the bangle to:
- Float
- Hover
- Sink deeply
- Pass through fabric
- Appear magnetically suspended
- Appear glued down
- Bend around fabric
- Deform because of folds

At contact points, the fabric may compress subtly beneath the bangle. The bangle itself must remain rigid.

6. NATURAL RESTING ORIENTATION

Allow the bangle to settle according to its geometry and center of mass.

It may:
- Lie partly on its side
- Rest at a slight angle
- Lean naturally against a fold
- Settle into a shallow depression
- Overlap another bangle when multiple pieces are present

Do not force a perfectly frontal orientation.

Do not make it stand vertically without support, balance impossibly on an edge, or appear ready to fall.

It should look as though the bangle was gently placed on the textile and naturally came to rest.

7. INNER OPENING & NEGATIVE SPACE

Preserve the exact inner diameter, shape and three-dimensional depth.

The opening must remain:
- Open
- Undistorted
- Free of invented jewellery
- Free of unnatural fabric intrusion

Fabric may be visible through the opening where physically appropriate, but must never pass through the solid metal.

Do not fill, close, flatten or redesign the opening.

8. FABRIC CONTACT & COMPRESSION

At real contact points:
- Fine wrinkles may flatten slightly.
- Fabric may compress beneath heavier areas.
- Small folds may rise around the outer edge.
- The textile may communicate the bangle's weight.
- Subtle contact shadows may appear.

Do not create exaggerated dents, bury the bangle, wrap fabric around its circumference, or make fabric appear attached to the jewellery.

The interaction must remain subtle and physically believable.

9. MULTIPLE BANGLES / STACKING

If multiple bangles are present, preserve the exact number, order, relative size, construction and arrangement.

Treat each bangle as an independent rigid object.

Each retains its own:
- Circular/intentional geometry
- Thickness
- Decorative structure
- Surface details
- Physical position

Adjacent bangles may touch or overlap naturally, but must not:
- Fuse
- Merge into one object
- Pass through one another
- Lose their individual openings
- Become visually indistinguishable

Stacked bangles should feel physically assembled, not digitally layered.

10. STACKING & WEIGHT

Respect natural weight distribution.

Heavier bangles may sit more firmly against the textile or adjacent bangles. Do not make every bangle float at identical elevation.

Allow small positional variation caused by gravity and contact while preserving the original stacking relationship.

Do not rearrange the source stack, add bangles to fill space, or remove pieces for cleanliness.

11. DECORATIVE & GEMSTONE FIDELITY

Preserve every source-supported:
- Stone and setting
- Prong/bezel structure
- Engraving
- Carving
- Groove/ridge
- Filigree
- Floral/temple motifs
- Repeated patterns
- Borders
- Relief
- Cut-outs
- Fine surface detail

Gemstones remain in their original settings.

Do not add/remove/change stone size, shape, color, placement or setting structure.

Raised elements remain raised; recessed elements remain recessed.

12. BAND SURFACE & CRAFTSMANSHIP

Preserve the original surface character, including ribbing, grooves, twisted metal, hammered texture, engraving, repeated ridges, carving, polished/matte sections, textured gold and handcrafted irregularity.

Do not smooth textured metal into generic polished metal, merge fine grooves, erase craftsmanship, or replace the source surface with uniform CGI geometry.

13. METAL REFLECTION & CONTACT SHADOW

Metal reflections must follow the actual 3D geometry of curved surfaces, ridges, edges, engravings, settings and relief.

Do not create flat uniform reflections or make metal appear liquid, plastic or artificial.

The bangle must create a subtle contact shadow where it touches fabric. Shadow direction must follow the universal base lighting.

No detached shadows, black outlines, halos, excessive darkness or contradictory shadows.

14. SCALE & COMPOSITION

The bangle remains the dominant visual subject.

Provide enough surrounding textile to establish physical context without making the bangle insignificant.

Do not enlarge beyond believable scale.

Do not crop through the main circular structure.

Keep the complete outer circumference and enough of the inner opening visible to communicate the product's three-dimensional form.

For multiple bangles, keep the complete group visible without cutting through important structures.

15. SOURCE PROXIMITY — ABSOLUTE

The wholesaler's input determines:
- Design
- Shape
- Size
- Proportion
- Quantity
- Decorative elements
- Gemstones
- Band structure
- Surface details
- Construction
- Arrangement

Do not use the source as loose inspiration.

Do not add luxury details, simplify difficult areas, or "complete" unclear jewellery.

When source information is unclear, preserve only the visible/source-supported structure.

NEVER INVENT JEWELLERY.

16. FINAL VALIDATION

Verify:
- Bangle rests naturally on soft velvet/suede.
- Bangle remains rigid.
- Gravity and center of mass are believable.
- Fabric responds subtly to weight.
- No floating, hovering, deep sinking or penetration.
- Original circular/intentional geometry is preserved.
- Inner opening remains open and accurate.
- Complete bangle/group remains visible.
- Band thickness, circumference and proportions remain unchanged.
- Decorative details and gemstones remain intact.
- Multiple bangles remain separate and physically believable.
- Original quantity and arrangement are preserved.
- No component is invented, duplicated, removed or redesigned.
- No price, pricing, barcode, SKU, label, watermark, seller marking, promotional text or commercial attachment remains.
- Final image remains extremely close to the wholesaler's original product.

FINAL RULE:

THE BANGLE IS THE PRODUCT.
THE TEXTILE IS ONLY THE PRESENTATION SURFACE.
THE BANGLE REMAINS RIGID.
THE FABRIC RESPONDS TO THE BANGLE.
THE WHOLESALER'S ORIGINAL JEWELLERY IS THE AUTHORITY.

PRICE TAG REMOVAL IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.
COMMERCIAL MARKING REMOVAL IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.
ORIGINAL PRODUCT FIDELITY IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.
SOURCE DESIGN MUST NOT BE REINTERPRETED.

WHEN UNCERTAIN:
PRESERVE THE SOURCE.
DO NOT INVENT.
