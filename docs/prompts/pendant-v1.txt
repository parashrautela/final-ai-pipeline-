 PENDANT

Apply these rules specifically to pendants while preserving all universal base-prompt rules.

IMPORTANT: THE PENDANT IS THE HERO PRODUCT. The chain is supporting context and must only be partially visible. Do not show the complete necklace when that reduces pendant prominence, scale or detail.

1. COMMERCIAL MARKING REMOVAL — ABSOLUTE

The pendant and its actual jewellery chain are the only approved product objects.

Remove ALL non-jewellery commercial material:
- Price tags/stickers/labels
- Pricing information/numbers
- Barcodes
- SKU/inventory/category labels
- Product cards/paper tags
- Seller/store markings
- Watermarks
- Website/social handles
- Promotional text
- Product codes
- Commercial logos not physically manufactured into the jewellery
- Strings/clips/holders used for identification

PRICE TAG REMOVAL IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.

No price, barcode, SKU, category marking, seller name, watermark, promotional text, website, social handle or commercial attachment may remain.

Never preserve, blur, hide, crop, relocate, recreate, replace or reinterpret commercial markings. Remove them completely. If removal exposes fabric/background, reconstruct only the minimum surrounding area. Never invent jewellery underneath a removed commercial element.

2. PENDANT HERO PRESENTATION — ABSOLUTE

The pendant must dominate the composition.

Prioritize the pendant body, central motif, gemstone, badge, medallion, deity/figurative design or decorative centerpiece.

Aim for approximately two-thirds of the visual focus/composition to be occupied by the pendant, with the remaining area providing controlled fabric and partial chain context.

The viewer must immediately understand: THIS IS A PENDANT PRODUCT.

Do not make the pendant a small accessory inside a full-necklace composition.

3. PARTIAL CHAIN VISIBILITY

Show only enough chain to establish physical attachment and context.

The visible chain should enter naturally from the upper area and connect directly to the actual bail/attachment point:

CHAIN → BAIL/ATTACHMENT → PENDANT

Do not show the full chain unless structurally necessary.

Do not let the chain dominate or disappear completely.

The chain remains secondary to the pendant.

The chain may extend beyond the frame, but the main pendant body must remain completely readable. Never crop through the main pendant, central gemstone, deity, badge, motif or major decorative structure.

4. TEXTURED VELVET / SUEDE PRESENTATION

Present the pendant against or resting naturally on premium soft textile.

Use:
- Velvet
- Suede-like fabric
- Fine brushed textile
- Rich tactile cloth

The universal base controls fabric color.

The fabric should contain subtle natural folds, wrinkles, ridges, compression and fine textile texture. Avoid perfectly flat synthetic surfaces and hard stone, ceramic, glass or rigid display surfaces.

5. PENDANT-TO-FABRIC PHYSICS

The pendant is rigid; the fabric is soft.

The pendant obeys gravity and the fabric responds to its weight.

When resting:
- Lower/rear contact areas sit naturally against the textile.
- Fabric compresses subtly beneath heavier sections.
- Small folds may form around contact points.
- A soft contact shadow establishes physical placement.
- Pendant must not float.
- Pendant must not deeply sink or pass through fabric.
- Fabric must not engulf or unnaturally wrap around the pendant.

The result must look like a real rigid pendant gently placed on velvet/suede.

6. ORIENTATION & CENTER OF MASS

Allow the pendant to settle according to its geometry and center of mass.

A slight natural tilt is acceptable, but the decorative front must remain sufficiently visible.

Do not:
- Flip it backward
- Rotate excessively
- Lay it completely face-down
- Hide the design
- Force it perfectly flat when geometry would prevent it

Large gemstones, deity figures, medallions or dense gold sections may influence resting orientation, but never exaggerate gravity or deform the original silhouette.

7. BAIL & CHAIN CONNECTION

Preserve the original:
- Bail
- Loop
- Connector
- Hook
- Chain attachment
- Upper pendant structure

The chain must connect directly to the actual attachment point.

Never attach the chain to an arbitrary location, embed it inside the pendant, create an unnatural gap, or invent a support.

The attachment must visually carry the pendant's weight.

8. CHAIN TENSION & GRAVITY

The visible chain behaves as flexible jewellery.

It must:
- Maintain believable downward tension
- Connect continuously to the pendant
- Form subtle natural curves where appropriate
- Respond to gravity
- Remain physically connected to the bail

Do not make the chain rigid like a graphic line, weightless, floating, unnaturally stretched or bent at impossible sharp angles.

The pendant's weight must be consistent with visible chain tension.

9. PENDANT DESIGN FIDELITY — ABSOLUTE

The central pendant is the highest-priority jewellery detail.

Preserve exactly:
- Silhouette
- Outer frame
- Central motif
- Deity/figurative design
- Ornamental design
- Gemstones and arrangement
- Engravings
- Filigree
- Borders
- Cut-outs/openings
- Relief depth
- Decorative cavities
- Fine metalwork
- Surface texture
- Internal negative space

Do not simplify, redesign, beautify, modernize or replace the design with generic luxury jewellery.

Do not add decorative elements not supported by the source.

The pendant must remain recognizably identical to the source product.

10. GEMSTONE & ORNAMENT INTEGRITY

Gemstones remain physically seated in their original settings.

Preserve:
- Size
- Shape
- Color
- Placement
- Prongs
- Bezels
- Surrounding metal
- Facet orientation

Do not enlarge, reposition, invent, remove or float stones.

Gemstones must reflect light according to their actual geometry.

Raised elements may create subtle self-shadows. Do not make stones appear printed onto the metal or buried inside it.

11. RELIEF & THREE-DIMENSIONAL DETAIL

Preserve distinct:
- Raised motifs
- Mid-level ornament
- Recessed areas
- Engravings
- Open cavities
- Borders
- Gemstone settings

Do not flatten multiple levels into one surface, fill openings, merge relief, or make recessed areas look painted on.

Lighting should reveal existing depth rather than create fake geometry.

12. FABRIC & SHADOW INTERACTION

Where the pendant contacts fabric:
- Fabric may compress slightly.
- Fine folds may flatten.
- A subtle contact shadow should appear.
- Shadow remains attached to the pendant.
- Fabric continues naturally around it.

No harsh black shadows, detached/floating shadows, excessive halos, deep artificial dents or shadows contradicting the base light direction.

If visible chain touches fabric, it must also follow the surface naturally and create subtle contact. If suspended, maintain a believable gap.

Never allow chain to pass through fabric.

13. CAMERA & PRODUCT READABILITY

Use a close, premium product-oriented composition.

Prioritize the pendant's front face, craftsmanship and fine details.

The pendant should occupy the dominant central or slightly offset area. The visible chain remains secondary.

Do not zoom so far that pendant edges are cropped or so far out that the pendant becomes insignificant.

The composition should clearly communicate:
- Pendant detail
- Scale
- Craftsmanship
- Partial chain relationship
- Fabric texture
- Physical contact

The final image should feel like a premium pendant product photograph, not a full necklace photograph.

14. FINAL VALIDATION

Verify:
- Pendant is the dominant product.
- Approximately two-thirds of visual focus is dedicated to the pendant.
- Only controlled chain is visible.
- Full chain is not unnecessarily shown.
- Chain connects to the actual bail.
- Chain tension is believable.
- Pendant responds naturally to gravity.
- Pendant rests naturally on velvet/suede.
- No floating, deep penetration or impossible fabric interaction.
- Original silhouette and central design are preserved.
- Gemstones remain in original settings.
- Relief, ornament and fine details remain intact.
- Nothing is invented, duplicated or removed.
- No price tag, pricing, barcode, SKU, category marking, seller marking, watermark, website/social marking, promotional text or commercial attachment remains.
- Pendant remains the unmistakable hero product.

FINAL RULE:

THE PENDANT IS THE PRODUCT.
THE CHAIN IS SUPPORTING CONTEXT.
THE FABRIC IS THE PRESENTATION SURFACE.

PENDANT-TO-FABRIC PHYSICAL CONTACT IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.
PARTIAL-CHAIN HERO COMPOSITION IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.
ORIGINAL PENDANT DESIGN IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.
COMMERCIAL MARKING REMOVAL IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.

WHEN UNCERTAIN:
PRESERVE THE SOURCE.
DO NOT INVENT.
