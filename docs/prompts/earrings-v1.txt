 EARRINGS

Apply these rules specifically to earrings while preserving all universal base-prompt rules. The source earrings are the exact approved products.

Earrings are wearable hanging jewellery. This module controls suspension, gravity, attachment, jhumka/dangle physics, stud presentation, hoop geometry, pair symmetry, component separation, weight distribution, and structural integrity.

1. COMMERCIAL MARKING REMOVAL — ABSOLUTE

Remove all non-jewellery commercial material, including price tags, price stickers/labels, barcodes, SKU/inventory/category labels, product cards, paper tags, seller markings, promotional text, printed numbers/codes, watermarks, website/social handles, commercial logos not physically manufactured into the jewellery, and tag strings, clips, holders or attachments.

PRICE TAG REMOVAL IS ABSOLUTE.

No price, barcode, SKU, commercial text, label, watermark, seller marking, or commercial attachment may remain.

Never preserve, blur, hide, crop, relocate, recreate, or reinterpret commercial material. Remove the complete physical tag and its attachments. If removal exposes background, reconstruct only the minimum surrounding area. Never invent jewellery hidden by a removed tag.

2. EARRINGS MUST BE SUSPENDED — ABSOLUTE

Earrings must be physically suspended, never placed flat on fabric, velvet, stone, wood, ceramic, tables, trays, cushions, or any horizontal surface.

Do not make earrings stand artificially.

Each earring must be supported from its actual physical attachment point: hook, ear wire, stud post, top loop, bail, or designed connector.

The jewellery must appear to hang because of its own construction and gravity.

3. SUSPENSION & GRAVITY

The upper attachment point is the primary suspension point.

From it downward:
- Hanging components respond naturally to gravity.
- Heavy elements pull downward.
- Flexible chains/connectors maintain believable tension.
- Lightweight elements follow their supporting structure.
- Hanging sections must not remain unnaturally rigid.

A slight natural resting angle is acceptable. Avoid excessive tilting, sideways twisting, impossible rotation, floating, or unstable suspension.

The final pose must look like a real earring hanging from its actual finding.

4. JHUMKA & DANGLING EARRING PHYSICS

For jhumkas, drops, chandeliers and other dangling earrings, preserve the physical hierarchy:

TOP ATTACHMENT → CONNECTOR → DECORATIVE BODY → LOWER BELL/MAIN DROP → DANGLERS/BEADS/PEARLS

Every component must remain connected to the component above it.

The jhumka bell/main drop hangs beneath its support. Beads, pearls, chains and danglers hang from their actual attachment points.

Allow small natural variations in angle and spacing.

Do not pull danglers sideways without physical reason, make them float, make chains pass through the bell, merge multiple danglers, force identical alignment, rotate hanging elements against gravity, or allow danglers to clip through each other.

5. STUD EARRING PHYSICS

Studs and compact earrings must also use their actual attachment mechanism.

Never place a stud flat on a surface.

Present it vertically or at a physically plausible slight angle using its actual post/finding while keeping the decorative face clearly visible.

The stud must look mounted or suspended, not placed.

Preserve the exact relationship between decorative face, gemstone setting, post, backing/finding, surrounding metal and connectors.

Do not enlarge the stud simply because it is small.

6. HOOP & CURVED EARRINGS

For hoops, circular earrings, chandbalis and other curved earrings, preserve the exact source-supported geometry.

A circular source remains circular. An intentionally oval source remains oval.

Preserve:
- Continuous curvature
- Original thickness
- Structural continuity
- Internal negative space

Suspend the hoop from its actual upper attachment point.

Never flatten, deform, or place it on a surface. Do not distort the opening to fit the composition.

For chandbalis and large curved earrings, decorative structures should follow the main frame while remaining naturally affected by gravity.

7. MATCHING PAIR

When two earrings are present, preserve them as the exact matching pair.

Maintain:
- Same scale
- Same proportions
- Same decorative arrangement
- Same gemstone placement
- Same construction
- Same product identity

Each earring must be independently suspended from its own attachment point.

Do not fuse them or make them appear attached to one invisible support.

Perfect mirroring is not required if natural suspension creates a slight difference in angle or height, but the pair must immediately read as matching earrings.

8. CONNECTION & COMPONENT INTEGRITY

Preserve the source-supported sequence of hooks, posts, loops, connectors, bails, chains, decorative bodies, bells, beads, pearls, danglers and drops.

Every component must remain physically connected.

No:
- Floating components
- Broken links
- Missing connectors
- Detached beads
- Merged chains
- Chains passing through ornaments
- Impossible intersections
- Duplicated components
- Invented attachment points

Fine chains and links must remain individually readable. Never turn delicate chains into thick generic metal strips.

9. WEIGHT & COMPONENT SEPARATION

Component weight must influence the resting position.

Large stones, heavy gold bodies, jhumka bells and dense ornamental sections naturally pull downward. Lighter chains, beads, pearls and small danglers follow the supporting structure.

Do not make heavy elements weightless or the entire earring unnaturally rigid.

Maintain clear three-dimensional separation between chains, bells, beads, pearls, danglers, drops and decorative frames.

Do not allow components to merge, clip together, collapse into one mass, or occupy impossible overlapping positions.

10. FINE DETAIL & SOURCE FIDELITY

Preserve all source-supported tiny details: beads, pearls, fine chains, filigree, engravings, gemstones, prongs, hooks, loops, borders, drops, cavities and relief.

Improve readability without changing construction.

Do not add or remove stones, beads, chains, danglers, decorative elements, or hidden craftsmanship.

Do not redesign the earrings or change gemstone size, shape, placement, decorative proportions, hook/post construction, or hoop curvature.

The source is the product, not inspiration.

11. DISPLAY & CAMERA

Present the earrings as clean, product-oriented suspended jewellery.

Keep the main decorative face readable.

Avoid extreme perspective or rotations that hide important details.

When a pair is present, both earrings must remain clearly readable.

Keep the complete earring—from top attachment to lowest significant element—visible whenever possible.

Do not crop product-defining sections.

The result must look like real jewellery hanging in a professional studio, not graphic objects artificially arranged.

12. FINAL VALIDATION

Before finalizing, verify:

PHYSICS
- Earrings are suspended, never lying flat.
- Each is supported from its actual attachment point.
- Gravity determines orientation.
- Heavy elements pull downward.
- Flexible components maintain believable tension.
- Jhumka bells and danglers hang naturally.
- Hoops retain original curvature.
- Studs remain properly mounted.

STRUCTURE
- Chains/connectors remain continuous.
- No floating, detached, merged, duplicated or invented components.
- No impossible intersections or clipping.

PRODUCT
- Original silhouette, quantity, gemstones and decorative structure preserved.
- Fine details preserved.
- No redesign or reinterpretation.

COMMERCIAL CLEANLINESS
- No price tag, price label, barcode, SKU, category/inventory marking, seller marking, watermark, website/social handle, promotional text, pricing information or commercial attachment.

FINAL RULE:

THE EARRINGS ARE THE PRODUCT.

THEY MUST BE PHYSICALLY SUSPENDED FROM THEIR ACTUAL ATTACHMENT STRUCTURE.

JHUMKA AND DANGLE PHYSICS MUST REMAIN NATURAL.

STUDS MUST BE MOUNTED, NOT PLACED.

HOOPS MUST RETAIN THEIR ORIGINAL CURVATURE.

PAIR IDENTITY MUST REMAIN INTACT.

PRICE TAG REMOVAL IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.
COMMERCIAL MARKING REMOVAL IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.
ORIGINAL EARRING FIDELITY IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.

WHEN UNCERTAIN:
PRESERVE THE SOURCE.
DO NOT INVENT.
