NECKLACE

Apply these rules specifically to necklaces while preserving all universal base-prompt rules. The source necklace is the approved product. This module controls drape, gravity, symmetry, chain integrity, pendant weight, dangling elements, surface contact and presentation.

1. COMMERCIAL MARKING REMOVAL — ABSOLUTE

The necklace is the only approved product. Remove all non-jewellery commercial material: price tags/stickers/labels, barcodes, SKU/inventory/category labels, product cards/paper tags, pricing/numbers/codes, seller markings, watermarks, websites/social handles, promotional text, non-product commercial logos, strings, clips and holders.

PRICE TAG REMOVAL IS ABSOLUTE. No price, barcode, SKU, commercial text, label, watermark, seller marking or tag attachment may remain.

Remove the complete tag and attachments; never preserve, blur, hide, crop, relocate, recreate or reinterpret them. Reconstruct only the minimum exposed background. Never invent jewellery hidden by a removed tag.

2. STRUCTURE & PRODUCT FIDELITY

Preserve the exact source necklace: silhouette, length, proportions, chain construction, decorative sections, central pendant/medallion, side motifs, gemstones, beads, pearls, danglers, connectors, links, relief, engravings, filigree, openings, texture and intentional irregularities.

Do not redesign, simplify, modernize, beautify, add, remove, duplicate, rearrange or make the necklace more symmetrical, elaborate, heavy or luxurious than the source. Do not invent missing motifs or details.

3. DRAPE & GRAVITY — ABSOLUTE

Preserve the source necklace's actual hanging geometry. For U-shaped necklaces, both sides descend naturally from their upper attachment points, forming a smooth continuous curve with the lowest point naturally near the center. Side sections transition gradually into the central section. No sharp bends, stretching, flattening, forced geometry or floating.

Do not convert a deep U into a shallow V or a V into a deeper U. Preserve source-defined depth, curvature and proportions.

4. WEIGHT & CENTRAL PENDANT

Respect real weight distribution. For a large pendant, deity motif, medallion or heavy central section, supporting chains should naturally lead into it; the central element should sit at the appropriate lowest point; supporting links must remain connected; the pendant must hang from its actual attachment structure; its weight may naturally influence nearby chain curvature; and beads/danglers below it must respond to gravity.

Do not exaggerate pull or deform the necklace.

5. SYMMETRY & REPEATED ELEMENTS

Preserve intended bilateral structure. Matching motifs, repeated elements, gemstones and sections retain source-supported placement, scale, spacing, orientation and construction.

Do not introduce accidental asymmetry or force mathematical symmetry onto naturally hanging elements.

6. CHAIN, LINK & CONNECTION INTEGRITY

Every chain, link, connector, bead, ornament and pendant attachment must remain physically connected. No broken continuity, floating/detached links, merged links, intersecting chains, chains passing through ornaments, impossible overlaps, duplicated connectors or missing attachment points.

Preserve original connection order and spacing. Keep fine links individually readable where visible; never turn delicate chains into one smooth metallic strip.

7. DANGLERS, BEADS & LOWER ELEMENTS

All hanging elements must respond naturally to gravity. Beads, pearls, chains, drops and danglers hang from their actual attachment points, maintain believable orientation and spacing, remain individually readable, and avoid clipping or merging.

Lower-edge danglers may follow the necklace curvature while still hanging downward. Do not rotate every dangler toward the camera or make elements point upward/sideways without structural reason.

8. DISPLAY & COMPOSITION

Present the necklace in a front-facing, product-oriented view that clearly exposes the complete design.

Do not twist it unnecessarily, hide side motifs, fold the chain over itself, compress layers, or reverse ornament orientation.

Keep the complete necklace readable from upper attachment points to the lowest decorative element. For long necklaces, provide sufficient vertical room in the square frame without compressing or cropping.

9. SURFACE CONTACT & SUPPORT

When displayed against a surface, the necklace must appear physically supported. Lower sections should follow the surface naturally; contact points should create subtle physical interaction; heavy sections should appear supported; chains must not pass through the surface; edges must remain clearly separated from the background.

Use subtle contact shadows only. Do not let shadows obscure fine detail. The necklace must feel physically present.

10. FINE DETAIL

Preserve source-supported filigree, engravings, tiny beads/pearls, gemstone borders, deity or figurative details, fine links, relief, open spaces and small dangling elements.

Improve readability without changing construction; never merge fine details, create artificial outlines, smooth chains into CGI geometry, or erase handcrafted character.

11. FINAL VALIDATION

Verify that the complete necklace, original silhouette, length, proportions, pendant, major sections, chains, links, connectors, gemstones, danglers and craftsmanship remain intact; weight and gravity are believable; intended symmetry remains; and no component is removed, duplicated, merged or invented.

Verify that no price tag, pricing information, barcode, SKU, label, watermark, seller marking or commercial attachment remains, and no removed commercial object has been reconstructed as jewellery.

FINAL RULE:
THE NECKLACE IS THE PRODUCT.
THE SOURCE DESIGN IS THE AUTHORITY.
PHYSICAL REALISM MUST SERVE THE SOURCE.
PRICE TAG REMOVAL IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.
COMMERCIAL MARKING REMOVAL IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.
ORIGINAL NECKLACE FIDELITY IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.
WHEN UNCERTAIN: PRESERVE THE SOURCE. DO NOT INVENT.
